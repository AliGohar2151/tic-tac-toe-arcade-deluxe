Game Modes & Rules

🎯 Classic Mode

The timeless 3x3 game. Get 3 of your marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line to win.

👻 Ghost Mode (Infinity)

No more boring draws! Each player can only have 3 marks on the board simultaneously. When you place a 4th mark, your 1st (oldest) mark disappears! Watch for the pulsating warning mark before it vanishes.

⏱️ Blitz Mode

You have only 5 seconds per turn! Make a quick move before the timer expires or a random square is chosen for you.

🔴 Unbeatable AI

Uses the mathematical Minimax algorithm. Can you outsmart it? (Hint: impossible in Classic mode, but maybe in Ghost or Blitz!)
