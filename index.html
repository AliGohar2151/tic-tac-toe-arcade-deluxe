Game Modes & Rules

🎯 Classic Mode

The timeless 3x3 strategic match. Form 3 of your marks in a line to claim victory.

👻 Chrono Ghost Mode

Strict FIFO decay! Each player may maintain at most 3 marks. When placing a 4th mark, your oldest piece flashes critically and evaporates.

⚡ Tactical Overdrive

Features 2 tactical power abilities per player:
• Shatter: Obliterate any opponent mark on the board.
• Phase Swap: Exchange a friendly mark with an adjacent enemy mark.

⏱️ Blitz Speed Timer

High-stakes time pressure! Fast monotonic turns with auto-move placement upon countdown expiration.

⌨️ Full Keyboard Navigation

Navigate the 3x3 grid with Arrow Keys, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, and place with Enter or Space.
